Existing CSR Works – NMDC

Existing CSR Works implemented by NMDC include development of key community and transport infrastructure assets such as an Anganwadi Centre with Kitchen at Patel Para, an Anganwadi Kitchen and Store at Vichha Para, construction of a CC Road at Sarpanch Para, and an Indian Overseas Bank–cum–Community Hall at Patel Para. These interventions have significantly strengthened social amenities and local connectivity within the village.
Executive Summary
  1. 01
    The Model Village Development Plan proposes a comprehensive set of 597 development assets across Natural Resource Management, Transport, Drinking Water, Social Infrastructure, Energy & Lighting, and Economic Infrastructure.
  2. 02
    Natural Resource Management (NRM) includes 56 WHS, 71 Check Dams, 7 LBCD, and 10 Pond Renovation works, aimed at groundwater recharge and climate resilience.
  3. 03
    Drinking Water Infrastructure strengthened through 116 Solar Water Tanks ensuring reliable and renewable water supply.
  4. 04
    Transport Infrastructure includes 11 Bridges, 25 Culverts, 84.11 km BT Roads and 39.91 km CC Roads (total 124.02 km).
  5. 05
    Social Infrastructure covers 12 Anganwadis, 12 Community Hall, 12 Mandap,11 Domesheds, 4 Handwashing Platforms, 3 Toilets, and 5.77 km Boundary Walls.
  6. 06
    Energy & Lighting interventions include 176 High Mast Lights and street lighting in 3 habitation locations.
  7. 07
    Economic Infrastructure supported through 5 Market Complexes.
  8. 08
    Overall interventions benefit 14236 people across 2854 households, aligned with NMDC CSR objectives.
